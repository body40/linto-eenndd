### Qualification Programs for International Professional HR Certifications 

### Qualification for international professional HR certifications represents one of LeadMinds’ strongest core pillars. International professional certifications are globally recognized credentials and accreditations that accelerate professional efficiency, elevate competence, and advance practitioners' career paths. Our training programs serve as preparatory and qualifying bootcamps designed to enable practitioners to master approved global HR standards, guided by the most prominent international professional models and certifications offered globally (including, but not limited to) :

- 
SHRM-CP | (SHRM Certified Professional): Certified HR Professional from the Society for Human Resource Management (USA).
- 
PHRi | (Professional in Human Resources - International): International HR Professional Certification from the HR Certification Institute (USA).
- 
sHRBP | (Strategic Human Resource Business Partner): Strategic HR Business Partner Certification from the Human Capital Institute.
- 
SPHRi | (Senior Professional in Human Resources - International): International Senior HR Professional Certification from the HR Certification Institute (USA).
- 
CHRM | (Certified Human Resource Manager): Certified HR Manager from the American Certification Institute.
Within this framework, we offer an integrated series of preparatory programs under the title (HRi Pro). This series is specifically designed as a qualifying methodology aligned with the bodies of knowledge of leading international organizations such as SHRM, HRCI, HCI, and ACI, aiming to develop the competence of professionals and advance them through three progressive levels:

### 🎯 Available Training Course Levels:

- 
HRi Pro - Foundation Level: A comprehensive 50-hour training program aimed at building a solid knowledge base for beginners in the field, preparing them to grasp the fundamentals and scope of foundational exams, such as the (aPHRi) certification and others.
- 
HRi Pro - Practitioner Level: An advanced 60-hour training program that focuses on operational aspects and globally approved professional practices. It is the ideal preparation for those targeting professional and mid-level exams, such as (PHRi) or (SHRM-CP).
- 
HRi Pro - Strategic Leader Level: A leadership-focused 70-hour training program that zeroes in on analytical skills, strategic leadership, change management, and organizational development. It aims to provide final preparation for the challenges of senior international certification exams, such as (SPHRi), (SHRM-SCP), and others.
Historically, our network of experts has achieved a success rate exceeding 80% in preparation journeys for professional HR certifications. However, LeadMinds does not guarantee passing any exam, as results ultimately depend on each participant's commitment, prior experience, study effort, readiness, and the requirements of the certifying body.

### Our Methodology: The LeadMinds Impact Framework

### From Training Delivery to Measurable Capability

The LeadMinds Impact Framework is designed to ensure that every learning solution moves beyond content delivery and creates practical value in the workplace.
Our methodology is simple, structured, and focused on impact. It helps clients define the need, design the right learning journey, deliver practical experiences, support application, and measure what matters.

### The LeadMinds Impact Framework

### 1. Understand

We start by understanding the organization’s goals, participant profiles, current capability gaps, and expected outcomes.
Outcome: Clear learning priorities and a focused development direction.

### 2. Design

We design a learning journey that fits the audience, business context, and desired capability outcomes.
Outcome: A relevant, practical, and fit-for-purpose learning experience.

### 3. Deliver

We deliver interactive and practical learning experiences using cases, discussions, tools, exercises, simulations, and guided practice.
Outcome: Stronger engagement, better understanding, and higher confidence.

### 4. Apply

We help participants apply tools, models, concepts, and techniques to realistic workplace situations.
Outcome: Learning moves from theory to practice.

### 5. Measure

We measure readiness, confidence, learning, application, and where possible, the practical impact of the program.
Outcome: Clearer evidence of progress and development value.

### 6. Sustain

We support continued growth through follow-up, coaching, mentoring, learning reinforcement, and practical action plans.
Outcome: Better continuity, stronger adoption, and sustained capability growth.
