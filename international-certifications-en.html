FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SHRM and HRCI?

SHRM certifications (like SHRM-CP) emphasize behavioral competency and strategic application. HRCI certifications (like PHRi) place more focus on technical HR knowledge and compliance structures. Both are globally recognized and highly prestigious.

What are the eligibility requirements for the PHRi exam?

For bachelor's degree holders, at least 1 year of professional HR experience is required. For non-degree holders, at least 4 years of experience are needed. We offer a free review to verify your eligibility profile.

Are international exam fees included in the prep course price?

Our prep course covers the training material, interactive platform access, and administrative support. The actual exam fees are paid directly to the certifying body (SHRM or HRCI) during scheduling, and we assist you with that process.

What is the language of instructions and the exam?

The official exam is in English (SHRM and HRCI offer Arabic options for certain levels). Our training lectures are conducted in Arabic, but all terminology, study slides, and question banks are kept in English to ensure you are exam-ready.
